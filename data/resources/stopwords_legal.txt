# Boilerplate of the Court of Cassation decisions: words present in (almost) every file,
# so their IDF is ~0 and they cannot separate documents.
# STARTING DRAFT built from the most frequent words of the corpus. It is refined with document
# frequency once the index exists (words in > 80-90% of documents are candidates).
#
# The analyzer removes these words AND every word with the same stem, so الطاعن / الطاعنين /
# الطاعنة need only one line. Because of that, never list a word whose stem is also a topic word:
#   NOT listed: بناء (construction), القانون (قانون التحفيظ العقاري), تاريخ (تاريخ التقييد),
#               الحكم, الطلب, الاستئناف (kept until the IDF analysis says otherwise).
